A track with a long intro isn't mixed in from where the beats of the rest of it start any more, which could skip more than half of the track. When the intro has a steady beat of its own, even one the rest of the track isn't on, the track is mixed in with its intro, on the beat. When the music starts more than 32 bars in otherwise, the track starts from the beginning right after the previous track's music ends. After the update the app finds the beats of about one in twelve tracks with a steady beat again in the background, and mixes by the beats it had until then.
