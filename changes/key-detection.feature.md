Find the key and the tempo of tracks that have none in their tags, and show them in the Key and BPM columns. The key is in Camelot notation, as Mixed In Key writes it: 8A, 9B and so on. The tempo shows for tracks with a steady beat, between 80 and 160 BPM. The app finds both in the background after a scan, the settings show how far it is. What the tags say stays as it is, and the files aren't changed.
